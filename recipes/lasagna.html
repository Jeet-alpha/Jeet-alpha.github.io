Lasagna

Home

Description

A classic, hearty homemade Italian lasagna with rich meat sauce and layers of melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound sweet Italian sausage
  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 16 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the sausage and ground beef in a pot until browned.
  2. Boil lasagna noodles in salted water until tender, then drain.
  3. Layer meat sauce, noodles, ricotta, and mozzarella cheese in a baking dish.
  4. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes covered, then 25 minutes uncovered.
